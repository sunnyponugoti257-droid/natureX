    Hardware behind each tile

    Each tile maps to one low-cost sensor module wired to one ESP32 board.

    TileSensor moduleSignalESP32 pin
    Water qualitypH probe with PH-4502C boardAnalog 0 to 5 VGPIO34
    Soil conditionCapacitive soil moisture sensorAnalogGPIO35
    TemperatureDHT22 (or DS18B20)DigitalGPIO4
    UV radiationGUVA-S12SD UV sensorAnalogGPIO32
    Radio wavesSmall RF energy detector moduleAnalogGPIO33
    Fire sensorIR flame sensor (KY-026 type)Analog and digitalGPIO36

    The pH board can output up to 5 V, but ESP32 pins accept 3.3 V, so put a two-resistor divider between the board and GPIO34. A cheap RF detector shows that radio energy is present, not what it is. In Demo mode the numbers are simulated. In Live mode they come from the ESP32 (Wokwi simulation or real hardware) through a public MQTT broker.

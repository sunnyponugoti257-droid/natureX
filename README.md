# NATUREX – Pancha Bhoothalu
### Five Elements. One Mission.

NATUREX is an IoT-based environmental monitoring system inspired by the five elements of nature: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space. The main aim of this project is to monitor environmental conditions, identify abnormal readings, and support timely action to protect our environment.

The system uses an ESP32 microcontroller connected to sensors to monitor soil moisture, water pH, temperature, humidity, air quality, light intensity, water-tank level, and movement. It classifies the collected readings into three categories: Normal, Watch, and Alert. Based on programmed conditions, the system can activate an alarm or automatically switch on an irrigation pump when the soil becomes too dry.

The project uses the Wokwi ESP32 Simulator to demonstrate its working and ThingSpeak for cloud-based monitoring of environmental data. Users can view sensor readings and charts through a laptop or mobile device.

NATUREX can be applied in smart agriculture, water monitoring, campus environmental monitoring, and sustainability education. In the future, it can be improved with mobile notifications, solar-powered operation, and additional environmental sensors.

**The main objective of NATUREX is to use smart technology to monitor nature, reduce resource wastage, and support a cleaner and more sustainable environment.**
